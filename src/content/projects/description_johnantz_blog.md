# Description of John's Blog

**Website:** https://blog.johnantz.dev/\
**Author:** John Antzoulis\
**Website type:** Personal technology and programming blog

## Overview

The blog has an exploratory, hands-on approach. The author enjoys
building things, understanding how they work, and sharing what happens
along the way---including the occasional mistake or debugging challenge.

## Main Topics

The blog covers several technology-related areas, including:

- **Web development:** Experiences with tools, techniques, and
  frameworks used to build websites and applications.
- **Programming:** Software development projects, experiments, and
  lessons learned.
- **3D printing:** Designing objects and preparing projects for
  physical printing.
- **Self-hosting:** Running and managing services independently.
- **Linux:** Exploring and working with Linux systems.
- **Technology in general:** Open-source software, task automation,
  new tools, and frameworks.

These topics describe the blog's overall focus and do not necessarily
correspond to separate site sections.

## Purpose

The blog provides a place to:

1.  Document ongoing learning and discoveries.
2.  Share solutions to technical problems.
3.  Record personal projects and experiments.
4.  Help readers who encounter similar challenges.
5.  Save other developers time by sharing useful findings and debugging
    solutions.

## Intended Audience

The site is aimed at people interested in technology, including software
developers, Linux and open-source enthusiasts, makers, and people who
enjoy personal projects involving 3D printing, self-hosting, and
automation.

Its practical, experience-based content may be especially useful to
readers looking for real-world lessons, troubleshooting ideas, and
inspiration for their own projects.

## Summary

John's Blog is a personal space for sharing knowledge and hands-on
experiences in web development, programming, Linux, self-hosting, 3D
printing, and other areas of technology. Its focus is on continuous
learning, experimentation, and practical documentation, with the goal of
making the author's discoveries useful to other people.
